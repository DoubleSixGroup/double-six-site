What we assess

Four dimensions of scale-readiness.

We read each one for a single question: is this business ready to reach scaled product-market fit — and what will it take to get there?

01

Brand

Does the brand earn attention, trust and a premium — and can it carry the business into new audiences and markets as it grows?

02

Product

Is there real, defensible product-market fit — and is the product built to deepen it at scale, not strain under it?

03

Technology

Can the stack and the engineering capability actually deliver the growth thesis — or is there a ceiling, a debt, a key-person risk hiding in it?

04

Cultural readiness

Is the organisation genuinely ready to scale — the leadership, talent, ways of working and appetite to execute at pace?
