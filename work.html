Selected work

Every engagement is led by Double Six operators — people who have built, rescued and scaled the thing they're assessing. Situation, problem, outcome — no slideware.

Private equity SaaS · 0→1

Building into demand, not into the ether

The problem

A well-funded private-equity data platform was building on assumption, not evidence. Product-market fit was asserted rather than proven, the roadmap was driven by a handful of voices, and investors wanted to see a return on their money.

The outcome

We defined and evidenced product-market fit from the market in, then rewired prioritisation so every roadmap decision traced to validated demand and commercial pipeline — a defensible articulation of fit, and the foundation to sell the product rather than perpetually rebuild it.

Telco · Category launch · 0→1

A late entrant that won on more than price

The problem

Entering a crowded, price-driven category years behind well-funded digital natives — with a legacy cost base and a business case resting on customer assumptions nobody had tested.

The outcome

We tested the assumptions to destruction, rebuilt targeting around real customer need rather than persona, and launched across two continents on an AI-native operating model built to scale to dozens of markets on a lean team — winning on trust, ease and reliability, not the cheapest megabyte.

Global insurance · Turnaround

A platform that was throttling its own growth

The problem

Every new client and market made a critical platform more expensive to run, not less. Outages, offline deployments and an unmanaged codebase had stalled a flagship international programme for well over a year.

The outcome

We rebuilt the roadmap around real business capability, installed release governance where there was none, and reset ownership across a complex global organisation. Scale stopped being a cost.

AI orchestration · Pre-raise

Brilliant technology nobody could buy

The problem

A genuinely novel AI product in a category with no established buying behaviour. Hard to explain, harder to price — and a funding raise depending on it.

The outcome

We turned the technology into a defined, priced, sellable proposition with an investor-legible milestone plan — and designed the product organisation to be built the day funding landed.
